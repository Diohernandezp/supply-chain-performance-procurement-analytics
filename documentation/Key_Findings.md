## Key Findings

The analysis of 1,000 shipment records highlights several patterns:

1. **Procurement spend:** Total spend is approximately $8.28M across the analyzed records.
2. **Delivery performance:** Overall on-time delivery (OTD) is 79.7%, with 203 late shipments.
3. **Late-spend exposure:** Approximately $1.57M, or 19.0% of total spend, is associated with late shipments.
4. **Supplier concentration:** The top five suppliers account for approximately 43.2% of spend; the top ten account for 76.1%.
5. **Transportation differences:** Air shipments show 100% OTD and average transit of 2.9 days, while Sea shipments show 61.4% OTD and average transit of 28.4 days.
6. **Lead-time variability:** Transit time averages 9.5 days, with a median of 6 days and a P90 of 24 days.
7. **Category concentration:** Machinery represents approximately 32.6% of total spend.

These findings are descriptive and reflect the sample dataset. They identify patterns for further investigation rather than proving causality or prescribing supplier decisions.
